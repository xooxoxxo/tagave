# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Music collectors managing a self-hosted archive, browsing albums and artists and checking identification, collection gaps, and metadata changes.

## Product Purpose

Liner catalogs and identifies music, connects local files to releases, and previews and applies recoverable tag corrections. It is a catalog, not a player.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, spacious. Roon is the reference for music-focused browsing and breathing room. Use direct, useful language.

## Anti-references

Dark or claustrophobic interfaces; repeated metadata pills; oversized statistics cards; confusing Library tabs that navigate into unrelated page structures; decorative or inconsistent controls.

## Design Principles

- Albums and Artists are distinct destinations with predictable navigation.
- Prioritize music identity; disclose maintenance and provenance separately.
- Show useful available data and distinguish missing information from zero.
- Keep controls familiar and consistent across screens.
- Preserve the compact, centered disclaimer on a subtly tinted bottom bar.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow the approved design specification: readable semantic colors, visible keyboard focus, responsive layouts, reduced motion, and accessible search and navigation. Target WCAG AA contrast; do not claim certification without an audit.
