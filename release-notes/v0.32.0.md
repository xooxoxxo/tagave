**Minor release (0.31 → 0.32).** The autotagger gets its screens: a four-step tab in Library care and its settings in Settings › Modules. The album page shows who decided its match.

## Before you update

- **Nothing to do.** No database change in this release.

## New

- **Library care › Autotagger:** four steps, Decider, Run, Spot check and Accept, shown while the module is on.
  - **Decider:** the local model with Train again, or the Jev key, cap and payload preview. A Jev run waits until the payload is marked Seen.
  - **Run:** In review, Weak, unidentified or Backtest; state, spent, projected, and the reason for a pause, with Resume and Cancel.
  - **Spot check:** your files beside the picked release, with Correct, Wrong and Unsure (keys C, W and U). A gate per band opens once its error bound is under your limit.
  - **Accept:** a preview with what cannot be undone and the nights the enrichment takes, then one undoable batch.
  - **Past runs and batches:** pick a run, or undo a batch. ([a00f850](https://github.com/xooxoxxo/tagave/commit/a00f850))
- **Settings › Modules › Autotagger:** Off/On, decider, key, model, monthly cap, maximum error rate, request limit, prices, spend and agreement per band. ([b734bab](https://github.com/xooxoxxo/tagave/commit/b734bab))
- **Who decided a match:** the album page shows it in the Identification fact and on the Activity tab. ([bbcf8d1](https://github.com/xooxoxxo/tagave/commit/bbcf8d1))

**All changes:** [v0.31.0...v0.32.0](https://github.com/xooxoxxo/tagave/compare/v0.31.0...v0.32.0)
